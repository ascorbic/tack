# Accessibility

The accessibility audit (#61, M5): what was checked, what was found and fixed, how it's kept fixed, and what's left. The user-facing side is on the docs site (`site/src/content/docs/boards/accessibility.md`).

## How it's checked

- **axe, in e2e** (`e2e/a11y.spec.ts`, `@axe-core/playwright`): WCAG 2.0/2.1 A and AA plus axe's best practices, in both themes, with reduced motion, over the home page signed in and out, `/setup`, a real board made from the retro template, every `/play` gallery and a dozen playground scenarios. Any **serious** or **critical** violation fails, with a short report of the rule, the elements and why. Moderate and minor findings are reported by axe but don't fail (the galleries' `region` and `landmark-*` findings are the galleries' own scaffolding).
- **Transient states are excluded**, by selector, because they aren't meant to be read: a widget row's placeholder while it's dragged out (`[data-lifted]`), a "since you were here" ring fading away (`.since-ring[data-fading]`) and the dependency map's dimmed nodes while another's path is traced (`.depmap-node[data-dim]`). Keep that list short and add to it only for states that last a moment.
- **Contrast of tokens** (`test/theme.test.ts`): every text/background token pair in both themes, plus label chips for GitHub's default label colours and the extremes (white, black, yellow), from the real `color-mix` percentages in `styles.css`. People's colours behind white initials are checked in `test/presence-colors.test.ts`.
- **Reduced motion** (`test/motion.test.ts`): every `animation` and `transition` duration in the client's stylesheets must be scaled by `--motion` (directly or through `--t-*`), with a short allowlist of durations that are real time rather than motion. The reduced-motion rule that stops looping animations is checked too.
- **Keyboard and screen reader behaviour** (`e2e/keyboard.spec.ts`): the skip link, the board's description, reading order, arrow keys, selecting, nudging and its announcement, Escape keeping focus, shapes out of view, leaving and re-entering the board, and O. The pure parts (reading order, nearest shape, shapes and boards in words) are unit-tested in `test/a11y.test.ts`.

## What was found, and fixed

**Keyboard canvas navigation.** React Flow makes shapes focusable, but Tab walked them in stacking order, skipped everything off screen (only shapes in view are drawn), wandered into links inside cards, and drew no focus ring (React Flow's CSS removes it). Arrow keys did nothing unless something was selected. Now (`src/client/navigation.ts`, `src/shared/a11y.ts`): a skip link, reading order for Tab (sections then their contents, lists in their order), arrow keys to the nearest shape (same section first, within 45° of straight ahead, else anything wholly past that edge), Home/End, panning to shapes out of view before focusing them, Tab into the board landing on the shape you were last on, Tab past the end leaving the board, a ring that springs into place at constant screen width, Enter/Space to select, O to open an item. Keyboard focus is kept apart from the selection, so the selection's keys (nudge, edit, rename, action bar, Escape) are unchanged; keys on a focused but unselected shape (`isOnUnselectedShape` in `keys.ts`) are never taken as the selection's. Escape keeps focus on the shape (React Flow blurred it).

**Screen-reader names and summaries.** Shapes were announced as "group, node" followed by everything inside them. Each node now has a role description ("sticky note", "card", "section", …) and is labelled by a hidden element (`ShapeLabel.tsx`) with its words, author, state and section, following live card data. Face-down silent stickies say only who wrote them, never their text (unless it's yours). The board region is named "Board" and described by a summary (`BoardSummary.tsx`), kept current. React Flow's help text says Tack's keys.

**Live regions.** Toasts were already a polite status region. Added an announcer (`announce.tsx`, one polite and one assertive region) for nudges, other people’s changes (gathered until a few seconds go quiet, never held more than eight; local edits and the board loading aren't said), and pushes that carry on after the push sheet's closed.

**Roles and names axe flagged** (serious/critical): the due-date calendar's grid had no rows and used `aria-pressed` on grid cells; the account menu held a radiogroup and a form (theme is now menu radios; dev sign-in sits outside the menu); history's list held a bare alert; the "Link an issue" field claimed to be a combobox controlling a list that doesn't exist; labelled spans without roles (CI dots, the snapshot shimmer, loading skeletons); widget rows were buttons inside list items with buttons and links nested inside them (the row's main part is now the button); scrollable regions with nothing focusable in them (widget bodies, the push review) couldn't be scrolled from the keyboard (`useScrollFocusable` in `a11y.ts` gives them a tab stop only while that's so); the actions gallery's static panels had no names.

**Contrast** (serious, both themes): three people's colours (green, sky, gold) were too light for white initials; label chips' text in light theme for light label colours (yellow, cyan); cursor chat names at 80% opacity; idle and away cursors' names faded on their colour (now an outline in their colour, readable); pill buttons that lightened their pill; and states that faded text below AA: paused rules, switched-off related groups (now struck through), waiting push steps, stale widgets (now desaturated, not faded), suspended installations, untitled sections (now italic), days outside the month in the calendar, settled history marks' flags, empty sticky mini cards in dark theme, the owner picker's "You" chip, the release draft's major bump chip in dark theme.

**Reduced motion.** `--motion` already scaled most durations, but with motion reduced (×0.001) looping animations (spinners, shimmers, the sync dot's pulse, the cursor chat caret) looped a thousand times faster, which reads as flicker. They now play once and rest (`animation-iteration-count: 1` under `prefers-reduced-motion` and the playground's reduced mode). Six durations weren't scaled at all (the board title's saving pulse, two shimmers, the sync dot, the inbox skeleton's stagger, `focusShapes`' pan); now they are, and a test keeps it so.

## What's left

- **Target size (WCAG 2.2, 2.5.8).** Not enforced: axe's `target-size` flags the notifications bell and account avatar (17px, 14px in the gallery's scaled frames), timeline dots (19px), the history panel's Bring back links (22px) and links inside cards (CI failure names, card titles). Most are dense data (a timeline, a card's CI list) where bigger targets cost legibility; the top bar ones should grow their hit area with padding.
- **Connectors** aren't reachable from the keyboard, and shapes don't say what they're connected to. A shape's name could end "connected to #412 and 2 more", and connectors could join the reading order after the shapes they join.
- **Inside shapes**: widgets' rows, a decision's votes and an action item's chips are reachable with Tab only once the shape is selected (Tab, past the action bar, into the shape). A keyboard way into a shape's contents straight from navigation (say, Enter on a widget) would help.
- **Comment pins** are buttons in their own layer, after the board in tab order, not in the shapes' reading order.
- **Dragging** has no keyboard equivalent beyond nudging: moving a shape into another section from the keyboard means nudging it there. Cut and paste works as a fallback.
- **Remote changes** are announced to everyone using a screen reader; there's no setting to quieten them, and they don't say who (the client doesn't know who made a remote Yjs update; history does, on the server).
- **Manual screen reader passes** with VoiceOver and NVDA haven't been done; the names and descriptions are checked in e2e through the accessibility tree, not by listening. Worth an hour before calling #61 done.
- **The minimap and zoom controls** are mouse-first; zoom has keyboard shortcuts, the minimap has none.
- **The docs site** (Starlight) wasn't audited here.
