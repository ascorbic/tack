## Settings

<WidgetConfig

	accepts={{
		from: "A date, `YYYY-MM-DD`, or empty",
		to: "A date, `YYYY-MM-DD`, or empty",
	}}
/>

**What to follow** narrows the search, usually to a repository (`repo:owner/name`). Tack adds the dates itself: `created:2026-09-27..2026-10-10` for opened items, and so on, plus `is:pr` when you plot merges. Relative ranges are whole days ending today, and move on each day.

## Using it

Markers stack by day, with density bars along the axis and a line for today. Where too many land close together they cluster ("12 items: zoom in"); click a cluster to zoom in, and **Show the whole range** to zoom out.

Point at a marker to see the item. Click it to find its card on the board, or open it on GitHub. Drag it out to add a card.
