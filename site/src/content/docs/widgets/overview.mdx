## Adding a widget

Press <kbd>W</kbd> (or **Widget** in the toolbar) to open the picker. Click a kind to add it in the middle of the view, or drag it onto the board. Clicking the board with the widget tool adds a blank list there.

The picker also has **Ready-made lists**, searches over the board's busiest repository (the one with the most cards): "Open bugs", "Needs triage", "Open pull requests", "Good first issues", "Assigned to me" and "My review queue".

## Configuring and resizing

The gear in a widget's header opens its settings beside it. Searches and text apply when you press <kbd>Enter</kbd> or leave the field, since each change runs the widget's searches again for everyone. Settings changes are undoable, like any other edit. **Done** or <kbd>Esc</kbd> closes the panel.

Select a widget to resize it from its edges and corners (at least 260 × 200). Pick it up by its header to move it. Zoomed out, each widget shows one big number.

Every setting of every kind is in its reference page, generated from the widget's schema.

## Whose permissions

**A widget's searches run as whoever's looking at it.** Two people looking at the same widget can see different rows.

- **Signed in**: searches run with your own GitHub account, so you see exactly what you could see on GitHub, private repositories included, and `@me` means you. Your results only go to you.
- **Not signed in** (on a public board): searches only look at public repositories (Tack adds `is:public`), and anything not known to be public is dropped. `@me` means nothing: "Sign in to see what “@me” means for you."

## When widgets refresh

- **On a schedule**, while anyone has the widget on screen: every 5 minutes for lists, timelines, review queues and milestones; every 10 for release drafts and dependency maps; every 15 for stale finders.
- **When GitHub says something changed** in a repository a widget searches (or whose items it's showing), from the [app's webhooks](/self-hosting/repositories/). Searches with no `repo:`, `org:` or `user:` run again on any delivery the board hears about. These refreshes wait a couple of seconds for more news, and never run a search more than once every ten seconds. Repositories without the app send no webhooks, so widgets over them refresh on the schedule.
- **When you ask**: the header's **Refresh**.
- **When its settings change**.

Results are shared: two tabs, or two widgets with the same search, cost one search. If a refresh fails, the widget keeps showing what it had: "Updated 10 min ago · couldn't refresh", with **Retry**.

GitHub limits how often anyone can search, so each board keeps well inside: at most 20 searches and 60 other reads a minute for each person (people who aren't signed in share one allowance). If a board needs more, its widgets wait: "Lots of searches on this board just now: trying again in a moment." If GitHub itself says to slow down, the widget says when it'll try again.

GitHub only searches up to 256 characters, and Tack adds a sort (and `is:public` for people who aren't signed in), so keep searches comfortably shorter than that.

## Rows and cards

Rows in every widget drag out onto the board as cards. The row stays, and the card remembers it came from the widget (see [Where cards come from](/automation/provenance/)). A pin marks rows whose item is already on the board; point at a row and its card lights up, and the other way round.
