## Making sections

Press <kbd>F</kbd>, then drag out a rectangle, or click to place one. A section drawn around shapes takes them in. Type its title straight away ("Name this section"); to rename it later, double-click the title, or select the section on its own and press <kbd>Enter</kbd>.

Sections come in grey, red, orange, yellow, green, blue and purple, from the colour picker that shows when one is selected. The badge beside the title counts what's inside.

## What's inside

A shape belongs to the deepest section that contains its centre and is bigger than it. Drop something in and the section lights up as it's about to land. Drag it out and it leaves.

- **Moving**: pick a section up by its title. Dragging inside a section draws a selection box over its contents instead.
- **Resizing**: drag one of the eight grips. Things stay where they are: shapes the section no longer covers leave it, and shapes it now covers join.
- **Nesting**: sections can sit inside sections, as deep as you like.
- **Deleting a section deletes everything in it**, with an undo toast.

## What a section can become

Select a section on its own (as an editor) and the bar under it offers **Parent issue** (<kbd>⌥</kbd> <kbd>⇧</kbd> <kbd>I</kbd>): one new issue, with everything in the section as its sub-issues. See [Built-in actions](/writing-back/actions/#parent-issue).

Its header offers:

- **Make inbox**: "Collect new matches for a search in this section". See [Inbox frames](/automation/inboxes/).
- **Silent mode**: "Everyone writes on their own: stickies stay face down until you reveal them". See [Silent mode](/facilitation/silent-mode/).
- **Vote**: "Dot vote on what's in this section". See [Dot voting](/facilitation/dot-voting/).

A [dependency map](/widgets/dependency-map/) can map the cards in a section, and a [rule](/automation/rules/) can add matching items to one.
