## Stickies

Press <kbd>S</kbd> and click the board, or drag a sticky straight off the toolbar button. The toolbar's colour picker sets the colour for new stickies: yellow, pink, blue, green or purple.

A new sticky starts in editing, ready to type ("Type something…"). To edit one later, select it and press <kbd>Enter</kbd>, double-click it, or just start typing. <kbd>Esc</kbd> or clicking away finishes. One stretch of typing is one undo step.

- **Author**: each sticky shows who wrote it, in their colour.
- **Text fits itself**: the type shrinks as you write more, so a short idea reads big and a long one still fits.
- **Style**: with stickies selected, the toolbar above them changes the colour, and the typeface between **Sans** and **Handwriting**, for every selected sticky at once.

Pasting text that isn't a link makes a sticky (up to 5000 characters).

A sticky can become an issue: select one or more and choose **Make issues** (<kbd>⌥</kbd> <kbd>I</kbd>). The first line is the title and the rest is the body. When it's pushed, the sticky turns into the new issue's card in place. See [Built-in actions](/writing-back/actions/).

In a section in [silent mode](/facilitation/silent-mode/), new stickies stay face down to everyone but their author until they're revealed.

## Text

Press <kbd>T</kbd> and click anywhere to start typing. Text has four sizes (**Small**, **Medium**, **Large** and **Extra large**) and the same two typefaces, from the toolbar above it. <kbd>Esc</kbd> or <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>Enter</kbd> finishes. Text you leave empty disappears.
