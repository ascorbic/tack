The **Facilitate** menu in the top bar (<kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>F</kbd>) has the tools for running a live session: a timer, spotlight, dot voting and silent mode, each a button. People who can edit the board can stamp; everyone, viewers included, can emote, chat at their cursor and high-five.

While anything is running, the Facilitate button pulses, and the menu starts with **Running now**: the timer, spotlight, open votes and silent sections, each with its controls (pause, +1 and stop; end; results, close and go there; reveal). It's one place to run the session from, and it tells someone who arrives late what's going on.

## Who runs what

Whoever starts something runs it: the timer, a vote, a spotlight or silent mode. They, and the board's owner, can change or end it; everyone else sees it but not its controls (a silent section says "Lena will reveal"). The server enforces this, so a stray click can't stop someone else's timer or close their vote. Once whoever started something has left the board, any editor can take it over, so nothing is ever stuck.

## Timer

Facilitate → **Timer** (or <kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>T</kbd>): say what it's for if you like ("Silent brainstorm"), then pick **1m**, **3m**, **5m**, **10m** or **15m**, or type the minutes and **Start**. Timers run from 5 seconds to 4 hours. Starting a new one replaces the old.

Everyone sees the same pill at the top of the board, counting down together: Tack keeps the time on the server and allows for each browser's clock, so everyone reaches zero at once, including people who arrive late. In the last ten seconds the digits pulse. At zero it says "Time's up", glows, and chimes once for everyone who saw it running. Turn the chime off with **Chime when the timer ends** in the Facilitate menu (remembered in your browser).

Whoever started it, and the board's owner, can **Pause**, **Resume**, add a minute (**+1**) or **Stop** it, from the pill or Running now. When they pause or stop it, everyone else is told ("Lena paused the timer"). "Time's up" settles into a small chip after 20 seconds, and tidies itself away after two minutes.

## Spotlight

Facilitate → **Spotlight me** (or <kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>S</kbd>) brings everyone to what you're looking at, and keeps them with you as you move: they follow your view, framed with your colour. People who arrive while you're spotlighting are brought along too.

Anyone can break out by panning, zooming, pressing <kbd>Esc</kbd> or choosing **Break out**, and **Rejoin** brings them back. The spotlight ends when you choose **Stop** (or press <kbd>Shift</kbd>+<kbd>S</kbd> again), or leave the board. One person spotlights at a time, and only people who can edit the board can spotlight. While someone else is spotlighting, the board's owner, or whoever's running the session, can **Spotlight me instead** or **End theirs**.

For a lighter "everyone, look here", **Call everyone over** moves nobody: everyone else gets a note ("Lena is calling everyone over") with **Go there**, which takes them to what you're looking at, once.

## People

Everyone on the board has an avatar in the top bar, and a colour that nobody else there has, kept for that board while it's free. Click an avatar to follow that person (as with spotlight, panning stops). Past eight people, **+N** opens a list of everyone here, with their status, who's following you, **Follow** and **Jump to**. When someone follows you, a small "Priya is following you" chip shows by your avatar.

## Stamps

Press <kbd>M</kbd> for the stamp tool, pick a stamp (👍 ❤️ ✅ ➕ 🎉 🔥 👀 ❓, or <kbd>1</kbd>–<kbd>8</kbd>), and click shapes to stamp them; click again to take yours off. Picking up the stamp tool puts down whatever was selected, so every click stamps; **Place dots** and the comment tool do the same. Stamps gather in each shape's bottom-right corner, with counts. Point at one, or tab to it, to see who stamped it: "👍 You, Priya Shah and Kai Andersen", you first, and past ten names "and 3 others". On a touch screen, press and hold a stamp.

Stamps are part of the board, so they stay. Only people who can edit the board can stamp.

## Volunteers

When the room is deciding who takes what, put your hand up rather than reacting or commenting. Point at an issue, a pull request or a sticky (or select it) and click ✋ (**I'll take it**): your face joins a **Volunteers** row on the card, or beside a sticky's author, for everyone to see. Click ✋ again to withdraw. Pointing at the faces names everyone: "Volunteers: You, Kai Andersen".

Volunteering is part of the board, not GitHub: nothing is assigned until someone stages it and pushes. **Assign volunteers** does that for a card's volunteers, and a sticky's go with it when it's made into an issue. See [Assign volunteers](/writing-back/actions/#assign-volunteers). Anyone signed in who can edit the board can volunteer.

## Emotes and high-fives

Press <kbd>E</kbd> for a wheel of emotes by your pointer, and pick one with a click or <kbd>1</kbd>–<kbd>8</kbd>: 👍 ❤️ 😂 🎉 👏 🔥 🤔 ✋. Each floats up from your cursor for everyone. The wheel stays open so you can send a few; <kbd>E</kbd> or <kbd>Esc</kbd> closes it.

✋ holds your hand out for six seconds, fading a little in the last two. When two people's raised hands meet, they high-five, and everyone watching sees it.

Emotes and high-fives aren't kept, and anyone on the board can send them, including people with view access.

## Pausing rules

If the board has [rules](/automation/rules/) that place cards, choose **Pause rules for this session** in the Facilitate menu so nothing lands on the board mid-discussion. Nothing is missed: what the rules would have placed waits, the top bar's **Rules** button counts it, and it's placed as soon as someone chooses **Resume rules** (in the same menu, or the Rules panel). The pause is for everyone on the board, and lasts until someone resumes.

## Cursor chat

Press <kbd>/</kbd> (or Facilitate → **Chat at your cursor**) and type: your words show by your cursor as you type, up to 100 characters. <kbd>Enter</kbd> sends, and it fades after a few seconds. Nothing is kept.

While a [dot vote with hidden results](/facilitation/dot-voting/#starting-a-vote) is on, everyone's cursors are hidden, and cursor chat with them, so nobody can watch where anyone votes. They come back when the vote closes.
