The **Facilitate** menu in the top bar has the tools for running a live session: a timer, spotlight, dot voting and silent mode. People who can edit the board can stamp; everyone, viewers included, can emote and high-five.

## Timer

Facilitate → **Timer**: pick **1m**, **3m**, **5m**, **10m** or **15m**, or type the minutes and **Start**. Timers run from 5 seconds to 4 hours. Starting a new one replaces the old.

Everyone sees the same pill at the top of the board, counting down together: Tack keeps the time on the server and allows for each browser's clock, so everyone reaches zero at once, including people who arrive late. In the last ten seconds the digits pulse. At zero it says "Time's up", glows, and chimes once for everyone who saw it running. Turn the chime off with **Chime when the timer ends** in the Facilitate menu (remembered in your browser).

People who can edit the board can **Pause**, **Resume**, add a minute (**+1**) or **Stop** it. A finished timer tidies itself away after two minutes.

## Spotlight

Facilitate → **Spotlight me** brings everyone to what you're looking at, and keeps them with you as you move: they follow your view, framed in your colour. People who arrive while you're spotlighting are brought along too.

Anyone can break out by panning, zooming, pressing <kbd>Esc</kbd> or choosing **Break out**, and **Rejoin** brings them back. The spotlight ends when you choose **Stop**, or leave the board. One person spotlights at a time, and only people who can edit the board can spotlight.

To follow one person yourself, without a spotlight, click their avatar in the top bar.

## Stamps

Press <kbd>M</kbd> for the stamp tool, pick a stamp (👍 ❤️ ✅ ➕ 🎉 🔥 👀 ❓), and click shapes to stamp them; click again to take yours off. Stamps gather along the bottom of each shape, with counts, and pointing at one shows who stamped it.

Stamps are part of the board, so they stay. Only people who can edit the board can stamp.

## Emotes and high-fives

Press <kbd>E</kbd> for a wheel of emotes by your pointer, and pick one with a click or <kbd>1</kbd>–<kbd>8</kbd>: 👍 ❤️ 😂 🎉 👏 🔥 🤔 ✋. Each floats up from your cursor for everyone. The wheel stays open so you can send a few; <kbd>E</kbd> or <kbd>Esc</kbd> closes it.

✋ holds your hand out for six seconds. When two people's raised hands meet, they high-five, and everyone watching sees it.

Emotes and high-fives aren't kept, and anyone on the board can send them, including people with view access.

## Cursor chat

Press <kbd>/</kbd> and type: your words show by your cursor as you type, up to 100 characters. <kbd>Enter</kbd> sends, and it fades after a few seconds. Nothing is kept.
