## Starting a vote

- **On a section**: Facilitate → Dot vote → **On a section…**, then click the section (or select it on its own first, or choose **Vote** in its bar). The vote covers everything in it, however deeply nested.
- **On the whole board**: Facilitate → Dot vote → **On the board…**

Then choose:

| Setting                          | Choices                                                                                                                                                                   |
| -------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Dots each**                    | 1 to 20 (3 to start with)                                                                                                                                                 |
| **Closes**                       | When it's closed, in 2, 5 or 10 minutes, after a custom number of minutes (1 to 480), in an hour, tomorrow, in 3 days or in a week. Short votes count down by the second. |
| **Hide results until it closes** | Others' dots aren't shown until then, "so nobody follows the crowd". In a session, cursors hide too (see below)                                                           |
| **Count 👍 on GitHub as votes**  | Each 👍 reaction on a card's issue or pull request counts once (off unless you turn it on, so what's popular on GitHub doesn't outvote the room)                          |

**Start voting**. Only people who can edit the board can start votes, and vote.

Hiding results is a courtesy, not a secret: the dots are part of the board.

While a vote with hidden results is on in a session (a quick one: closing in under an hour, or open until it's closed and started less than an hour ago), everyone's cursors are hidden, the facilitator's included, so nobody can watch where someone clicks. Your pointer isn't sent at all until the vote closes or its time runs out, and the vote's bar says "Cursors hidden while voting". People's pictures stay in the top bar. A hidden vote that runs an hour or more leaves cursors alone.

## Voting

The vote's bar at the top of the board shows how many dots you have left and when it closes. Choose **Place dots**, then click stickies, cards, text or decisions to put dots on them. You can put several on one thing. Click one of your dots to take it back.

A shape's dots go to the closest vote around it: a section's vote if it's in one, otherwise the board's.

If a vote closes within a day and you still have dots, Tack reminds you once: "Voting closes in 3h: you have 2 dots left".

## Results

**Results** ranks everything by its dots (ties share a rank), with who voted and, when 👍 count, how many reactions. Click a row to find it on the board.

With 👍 counting, each reaction on GitHub is a vote, but someone who reacted and also put dots on the card here is only counted once, by their dots. GitHub doesn't send webhooks for reactions, so new ones arrive the next time the card is fetched.

## Closing

A vote closes when someone chooses **Close vote**, or when its time runs out. Whoever started it, and the board's owner, can close it or **Reopen…** it (until closed, or for 2, 5 or 10 minutes, an hour, a day, three days or a week), and **Delete vote…**, which asks first since everyone's dots go with it. If whoever started it has left the board, anyone who can edit it can close or reopen it. The server puts right anything else, so a stray click can't end someone's vote.

When a vote closes, its results open for everyone who's there, and each shape that got dots keeps a badge with its rank and total (the top three stand out) until you put the vote away. Choose ✕ on a closed vote's bar to put it away, in your browser. While another vote is open, closed ones shrink to small results chips.
