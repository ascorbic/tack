:::note[Coming soon]
Board templates aren't in Tack yet. They're planned: ready-made boards for a **retro**, **triage**, **release planning**, a **roadmap** (Now, Next, Later) and an **incident review**.
:::

Until then, a few things come close:

- **Copy a board you like.** Select everything (<kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>A</kbd>), copy, and paste it onto a new board: sections, stickies, widgets and connectors all come along.
- **Ready-made lists**: the widget picker (<kbd>W</kbd>) has searches for open bugs, issues that need triage, open pull requests, good first issues, what's assigned to you and your review queue. See [Widgets](/widgets/overview/#adding-a-widget).
- **Inbox starting points**: [Make inbox](/automation/inboxes/) offers searches for untriaged issues, new bugs, good first issues and open pull requests.
- **Ready-made rules**: an empty rules panel offers [Pull requests beside their issues](/automation/rules/) and New matches into a section.
